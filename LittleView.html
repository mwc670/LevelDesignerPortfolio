A 3D Cozy Puzzler Level

Little View started as a simple GameJam project that has since blossomed into a short demo. My focus for this artifact was to leverage elements of the game's environment as solutions for a few cozy puzzles to demonstrate what I since learned about Unreal Engine's toolsets. The updated video shows how I started to advance in key aspects such as lighting, sequences, and interactive elements to engage the player better. This is evident in the opening and closing scenes, the moving dig piles, and the addition of a wind force for the final approach. One of the challenging aspects to this refinement was understanding the build process of a previous teammate that in the first interaction focused on the player character. Being able to build upon their previous structure so that I could get the player character to the level I needed for this playthrough took some time and adjustments to existing code, but I was able to effectively repurpose several of the interactions. Ultimately, my goal was to make this project more appealing to potential players, and I believe this overview will show that the update provides this enhancement:
