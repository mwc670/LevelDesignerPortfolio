A Stealth Level

Blend Out is a refinement of one of my first ever projects made in Unreal Engine. My initial inspiration for this project was to build a level focusing on a character's quick exit from a house party. After combining some of my Blender work with assets from Synty Studios and ithappy, I was able to make a simple house layout that the player could navigate through utilizing a color matching technique in order to reach their end destination. This artifact shows my early growth and understanding of NPC awareness systems, object placement, and how character direction can be used in crafting a playful tone. The original video provided in the side by side shows the more crude figures and blocking, whereas the updated video takes advantage of better player cues and a more recognizable set of scenery.
