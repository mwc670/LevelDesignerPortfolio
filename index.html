Artifact One

Blend Out: A Stealth Level Design

This artifact is a refinement of one of my first ever projects made in Unreal Engine. My initial inspiration for this project was to build a level focusing on a character's quick exit from a house party. After combining some of my Blender work with assets from Synty Studios and ithappy, I was able to make a simple house layout that the player can navigate through utilizing a color matching technique to reach their end destination. I included this artifact, as while it is not anything advanced, it shows how my understanding of NPC awareness systems, object placement, and character direction can be used to help craft a playful tone. While the original shows more crude figures and blocking, the updated video takes advantage of better player cues and a more recognizable set of scenery.

Artifact Two

Drips of Time: A 2D Platformer Design

My second artifact is a design that began several years ago as part of an online tutorial that I have since developed into a concept I have always wanted to explore in more detail: a character that is able to slips through time and enter multiple settings. I enjoy this idea because it allows me the creativity to blend multiple types of environments but still requires me to hold a solid story and design thread that keeps everything connected. This level design leans on a "biome" type of organization, where certain enemies and hazards have been made to fit their respective settings. As you will see in the original video, I started with only one enemy from this lesson that did not attack or chase the player, and two hazards that looked the same throughout each iteration. Since refinement began, I have added in more enemies that pose actual threats to the player, whether that be through tracking and speed, or via long-range attacks. I have also added in more hazards, like a lava splash, that draws upon the different tile patterns. The result is a more expansive experience for the player that better expresses the storline through the environment.

Artifact Three

Little View: A 3D Cozy Puzzler

My third artifact started as a simple GameJam project that has since blossomed into a short demo. My focus for this artifact was to leverage elements of the game's environment as solutions for a few cozy puzzles to demonstrate what I since learned about Unreal Engine's toolsets. The updated video shows how I started to advance in key aspects such as lighting, sequences, and interactive elements to engage the player better. This is evident in the opening and closing scenes, the moving dig piles, and the addition of a wind force for the final approach. One of the challenging aspects to this refinement was understanding the build process of a previous teammate that in the first interaction focused on the player character. Being able to build upon their previous structure so that I could get the player character to the level I needed for this playthrough took some time and adjustments to existing code, but I was able to effectively repurpose several of the interactions. Ultimately, my goal was to make this project more appealing to potential players, and I believe this overview will show that the update provides this enhancement:

Artifact Four

Willville: A Narrative 3D Adventure

Willville is a project that I started for Synty Game Jam that I have continued to iterate into a narrative demo. This project was my first time using Pixel Crushers Dialogue Manager, and I wanted to have a sample that showcase my ability to construct overlapping storylines. In Willville, I am able to utilize minigames and NPC dialoguea to build a story about a small town Mayor trying to finish last minute items before a town celebration. Constructing this narrative required quest mapping, dialogue trees, and generating responsive bark dialogue to enhance the player experience. The updated video will also show the growth in building out scene lighting and terrain costruction despite the more limited WebGL build. Building Willville required a fair number of C# scripts to make the minigames and quest system align, but with this structure it can also be easily expanded into longer play experience.
