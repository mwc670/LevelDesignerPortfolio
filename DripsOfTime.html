Drips of Time

Main Page Drips of Time

A 2D Platformer Slice

Drips of Time is a design that began several years ago as part of an online tutorial that I have since developed into a concept I have always wanted to explore in more detail: a character that is able to slips through time and enter multiple settings. I enjoy this idea because it allows me the creativity to blend multiple types of environments but still requires me to hold a solid story and design thread that keeps everything connected. This level design leans on a "biome" type of organization, where certain enemies and hazards have been made to fit their respective settings. As you will see in the original video, I started with only one enemy from this lesson that did not attack or chase the player, and two hazards that looked the same throughout each iteration. Since refinement began, I have added in more enemies that pose actual threats to the player, whether that be through tracking and speed, or via long-range attacks. I have also added in more hazards, like a lava splash, that draws upon the different tile patterns. The result is a more expansive experience for the player that better expresses the storyline through the environment.

✦ Design Highlights ✦
  • Environmental Storytelling: Allowing the art of the scene to dictate to the player a significant portion of the information.
  • Risk/Reward Setup: Designing level pathways that provide the player with opportunites for extra coins at a cost.
  • Enemy Variability: Created enemies that hurt the player in different ways: touch, breath, and long range.
