A Narrative 3D Adventure Slice

Willville is a project that I started for Synty Game Jam that I have continued to iterate into a narrative demo. This project was my first time using Pixel Crushers Dialogue Manager, and I wanted to have a sample that showcase my ability to construct overlapping storylines. In Willville, I am able to utilize minigames and NPC dialoguea to build a story about a small town Mayor trying to finish last minute items before a town celebration. Constructing this narrative required quest mapping, dialogue trees, and generating responsive bark dialogue to enhance the player experience. The updated video will also show the growth in building out scene lighting and terrain costruction despite the more limited WebGL build. Building Willville required a fair number of C# scripts to make the minigames and quest system align, but with this structure it can also be easily expanded into longer play experience.
